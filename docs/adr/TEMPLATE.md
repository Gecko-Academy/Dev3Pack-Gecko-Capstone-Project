# Title: the decision, in one line

*Say what was decided, not what the topic was. "No retry after a paid call", not
"retries".*

## Status and date

*One of proposed, accepted, superseded by `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`. Plus the date you
decided. Once it says accepted, this file stops changing.*

## Context

*What forced this. The situation, the constraint, the thing that broke. Facts only, and
a number if you have one. No solutions here.*

## Decision

*What you are doing, in the present tense, as a rule somebody could follow without you
in the room.*

## What this forbids

*The specific things that are now out of bounds. If you cannot fill this in, you have
not decided anything yet.*

## Alternatives and what they cost

*What else you could have done, and the reason you did not. One line each. The reader
who disagrees with you is looking straight at this section.*

## Reversibility

*One-way or two-way door. If two-way, say what it would cost to walk back. If one-way,
say what makes it one-way.*

## Consequences

*What gets easier, what gets harder, what you will have to live with. Include the bad
ones. A record with only good consequences was written to win an argument.*
